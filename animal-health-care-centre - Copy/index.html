Our history

From Prime Animal Healthcare Consultancy Limited to AHC.

Prime Animal Healthcare Consultancy Limited is a Nigerian-owned agricultural and animal-health enterprise established on 6 August 2017 with the vision of providing accessible, reliable and professional solutions to livestock farmers, animal owners and other stakeholders within the agricultural value chain. The company operates under the Animal Health Care Centre Ltd (AHC) brand, with its headquarters at 234-236 Murtala Muhammed Way, Benin City, and several other outlets in Benin City, Edo State.

From its modest beginning, the company has progressively expanded from animal-health-related services into a broader agricultural enterprise. Its activities include the sale and distribution of veterinary drugs, vaccines, animal feeds, livestock, feed ingredients, livestock equipment and other agricultural products, alongside animal-health consultancy, animal treatment, milling and feed production, farm advisory services and professional training.

Over the years, Prime Animal Healthcare Consultancy Limited has strengthened its presence within the livestock and agricultural sector by supporting farmers with practical knowledge, quality products and technical services. The company has also provided training opportunities for students, National Youth Service Corps members, trainees and other young people seeking practical experience in animal agriculture.

The company has grown under the leadership of its Managing Director, David Oluwaseun Bamigboye, a Registered Animal Scientist. His professional background and interest in animal production, nutrition, health and agribusiness have contributed to the company's development. In recent years, the company has gained recognition within the agricultural community, including public recognition of Bamigboye as a Young Agro Innovator, an invitation to engage with a governor, participation in discussions on climate-smart livestock production and stakeholder meetings organized by the Federal Ministry of Livestock Development (FMLD).

Today, Prime Animal Healthcare Consultancy Limited continues to evolve as an integrated animal-health and agribusiness company, serving farmers and other customers while promoting improved animal health, better livestock productivity, sustainable farming practices and access to agricultural products and professional knowledge.
